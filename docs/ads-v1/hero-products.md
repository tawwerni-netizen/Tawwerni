# HERO PRODUCT SELECTION & SCORECARD (TAWWERNI ADS V1)

## 1. Methodology & Evaluation Criteria
To avoid subjective bias ("I think coding is best" or "AI sounds cool"), every potential Hero Product is evaluated across 8 commercial and educational dimensions on a 10-point scale:

1. **Market Demand (DMD)**: Search volume, conversational buzz, cultural relevance in Egypt.
2. **Problem Clarity (PRB)**: Is the pain immediately obvious to the customer in a 3-second ad hook?
3. **Outcome Clarity (OUT)**: Can the learner clearly envision the deliverable they will build?
4. **Day 1 Strength (D1S)**: Does Day 1 provide immediate utility and a high-satisfaction "aha!" moment?
5. **Product Proof (PRF)**: How easily can the product and interface be demonstrated in vertical video?
6. **Low Purchase Friction (FRC)**: Low mental resistance to investing 59 EGP for this capability.
7. **Repeat Purchase Engine (RPT)**: Does mastering this skill naturally trigger the need for another?
8. **Career Path Bridge (CPB)**: Does this track anchor a full 149 EGP Career Path roadmap?

## 3. Deep Dive: Hero Product #1 — Prompt Engineering Mastery

### Verified Platform Metadata:
* **Track ID**: `Track #1`
* **Canonical Slug**: `prompt-engineering-mastery`
* **Official Title (AR)**: هندسة الأوامر المتقدمة (Prompt Engineering)
* **Official Title (EN)**: Advanced Prompt Engineering
* **Pillar**: الذكاء الاصطناعي وهندسة الأوامر (Pillar #1)
* **Academic Level**: مبتدئ (Beginner)
* **Total Curriculum**: 28 Lessons · 5 Hours · 2,400 XP
* **Commercial Pricing**: **59 EGP / 365 Days** (One-time, no subscription)
* **Free Trial Experience**: **Day 1 Free** without signup barrier
* **Linked Career Path**: `ai-automation-specialist` (أخصائي ذكاء اصطناعي وأتمتة) — Position: **Starting Anchor Track**

### Why This Product Wins The First Acquisition Test:
1. **Universal Relevance**: Anyone using a smartphone or computer today has attempted to use ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. The market size is not restricted to programmers or designers.
2. **Immediate Pain Relief**: Almost every user suffers from the "inconsistent output syndrome" — asking AI for something, receiving mediocre generic answers, and giving up or manually rewriting it.
3. **High Product Proof**: Showing a side-by-side prompt demonstration in a 15-second vertical video is visually visceral and requires zero technical jargon to understand.
4. **Instant Day 1 Gratification**: Day 1 teaches the difference between probabilistic guessing and structured prompt frameworks, giving the learner an immediate usable template in under 10 minutes.
5. **No Equipment Barrier**: Requires no high-end GPU, no code IDE, no software licenses. A free web browser or phone is sufficient.

### Real Day 1 Mission Structure:
* **Lesson 1 Title**: *إيه هو الذكاء الاصطناعي فعلاً؟*
* **Core Mental Model**: Moving from conversational chat ("سؤال وجواب كأنك بتكلم صاحبك") to deterministic instruction architecture (RTCC: Role, Task, Context, Constraint).
* **Mission 1 Objective**: فهم طبيعة النماذج اللغوية الكبيرة كأداة احتمالية وإتقان صياغة الأوامر المهيكلة.
* **Mission 1 Deliverable**: برومبت تنفيذي متقدم (RTCC / Few-Shot) مع قيود إخراج وسياق لمنع الهلوسة.
* **Golden Standard Model**: مصفوفة برومبت متكاملة تحتوي على السياق المحدد، والشخصية الاعتبارية، والشروط السلبية (Negative Constraints)، والأمثلة التوضيحية (Few-Shot).

### Capstone Portfolio Deliverable:
* **Final Deliverable (Day 28)**: *Megaprompt Architecture Deck* — A documented suite of 5 custom, production-grade automated prompts for business reporting, research synthesis, and creative ideation.
